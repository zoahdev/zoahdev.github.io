WHAT THIS PROVES

Cryptographic integrity is not physical truth.

The receipt verifier proves signature integrity, content addressing, required fields, and—only when you provide a matching trust anchor—executor trust. A receipt remains an executor attestation; it does not independently prove that a physical action occurred.
